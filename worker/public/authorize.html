This app wants to use your Skwirl.

  • Search and read your saved notes
  • See your lists of books, movies and places
  • Save new links you give it, and mark notes as used

It can't delete anything or see your sign-in link. You can disconnect it anytime by resetting your sign-in link in Set up.
