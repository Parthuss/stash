How to search your saved Instagram reels

By Parth Akholkar, who makes Skwirl · 8 November 2026

You saved it for a reason: a recipe, a book, a gym tip, a place to visit. Months later you remember one word from it and you can't find it. That's because, for most people, Instagram's Saved tab has no search box (true as of November 2026). You can only scroll.

Option 1: collections (built in, but manual)

  1. Open your profile, tap the menu (☰), then Saved.
  2. Tap + to make a collection, like "Recipes" or "Books".
  3. From then on, when you tap save on a post, use the Save to collection option that pops up to drop it into one.

Collections help you browse, but you still can't search inside them, and you have to sort every save by hand.

Option 2: make each save searchable with Skwirl

Skwirl reads each reel you share to it, the speech, the text on screen and the caption, and writes a short note. Then you search your notes, not a grid of thumbnails.

  1. Open Skwirl and start free (no password, no card).
  2. In Instagram, tap Share on a reel, then pick Skwirl from the share menu. On iPhone that's a one-tap Shortcut; on Android you install Skwirl from Chrome. Or copy the link and paste it in.
  3. A couple of minutes later it's a note. Search it by anything that was said: "that pasta with lemon", "the book about habits".

Recipes come back as ingredients and steps. Books, films and places land in one list you can tick off. If you use ChatGPT or Claude, you can connect them and just ask: "what was that recipe I saved last month?"
