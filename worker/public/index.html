Questions

What is Skwirl?

Skwirl turns the Instagram reels and videos you save into notes you can search. Share a reel to Skwirl and a couple of minutes later it's a note: recipes come back as ingredients and steps, books, films and places land in one list, and tips come with the next step. ChatGPT and Claude can read your notes too. It's free.

Can I search my saved Instagram reels?

Instagram's Saved tab has no search box for most people. Skwirl reads each reel you share to it (the speech, the on-screen text and the caption) and turns it into a note, so you can search everything you saved by what was said in it.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone (through a Shortcut, Skwirl walks you through it), Android (install it from Chrome and it shows up in the share menu), and any browser, where you can paste links. You can also send links to the Skwirl bot on Telegram.

How long does a save take?

Usually a couple of minutes. You'll see it appear in your library with a "Reading…" card while it works.

Who can see my saves?

Only you, unless you share a note. Skwirl never re-posts the video, it keeps a short note and a small cover image. Details in the privacy policy.

Can I bring in reels I saved before?

Yes. Download your Saved posts from Instagram (Accounts Center, Export your information, JSON format) and pick the file in Skwirl. It's read on your device and only the post links are sent. Step-by-step guide.

There's no password. How do I get back in?

You get a private sign-in link. Skwirl asks you to save it (email it to yourself, or keep it in your notes). Open it on any device and you're in.

What's a Groq key and do I need one?

No. Groq runs the AI that reads reels. Skwirl covers it for you. If you save a lot, a free key of your own means you're never queued behind anyone.

Good to know
Limits, timing, privacy

    

      
What can I save? Reels and videos from Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and X. TikTok and Threads aren't supported yet.

      
How long does it take? Usually a couple of minutes. When lots of people are saving, or Skwirl's processing computer is offline, it can take a few hours. Nothing gets lost, it waits in line, and you'll see a banner if it's slow.

      
Limits. 25 saves waiting at once, and 30 a day on the shared AI allowance. Add your own free Groq key above and it's 200 a day.

      
Why did a save fail? Usually the post is private or deleted, or YouTube blocked the download. Tap Retry once. If it fails again, remove it.

      
Who can see my saves? Only you, in the app. But whoever runs Skwirl can read the database. Don't save anything you'd hate for them to see.

      
Leaving? Export your notes or delete your account from your profile (the round button at the top). Deleting is permanent.
