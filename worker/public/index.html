Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Everyone shares a free AI allowance, about 30 saves a day each. Want more? Add your own free Groq key in two minutes and get 200 a day.

Why not just use Instagram's Saved folder?

Saved is a grid of thumbnails you can't search. Stash turns each post into text, so "that pasta thing" or "the book the guy in the car mentioned" actually finds it. It also works across apps, not only Instagram.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone (through a Shortcut, Stash walks you through it), Android (install it from Chrome and it shows up in the share menu), and any browser, where you can paste links.

Who can see my saves?

Only you, unless you share a note. Stash never re-posts the video, it keeps a short note and a small cover image. Details in the privacy policy.

There's no password. How do I get back in?

You get a private sign-in link. Stash asks you to save it (email it to yourself, or keep it in your notes). Open it on any device and you're in.

Good to know
Limits, timing, privacy

    

      
What can I save? Links from Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X and Threads.

      
How long does it take? Usually a couple of minutes. When lots of people are saving, or Stash's processing computer is offline, it can take a few hours. Nothing gets lost, it waits in line, and you'll see a banner if it's slow.

      
Limits. 25 saves waiting at once, and 30 a day on the shared AI allowance. Add your own free Groq key above and it's 200 a day.

      
Why did a save fail? Usually the post is private or deleted, or YouTube blocked the download. Tap Retry once. If it fails again, remove it.

      
Who can see my saves? Only you, in the app. But whoever runs Stash can read the database. Don't save anything you'd hate for them to see.

      
Leaving? Export your notes or delete your account under Your account. Deleting is permanent.
