Questions

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone (through a Shortcut, Skwirl walks you through it), Android (install it from Chrome and it shows up in the share menu), and any browser, where you can paste links.

How long does a save take?

Usually a couple of minutes. You'll see it appear in your library with a "Reading…" card while it works.

Who can see my saves?

Only you, unless you share a note. Skwirl never re-posts the video, it keeps a short note and a small cover image. Details in the privacy policy.

There's no password. How do I get back in?

You get a private sign-in link. Skwirl asks you to save it (email it to yourself, or keep it in your notes). Open it on any device and you're in.

What's a Groq key and do I need one?

No. Groq runs the AI that reads reels. Skwirl covers it for you. If you save a lot, a free key of your own lifts your limit to 200 a day.

Good to know
Limits, timing, privacy

    

      
What can I save? Links from Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X and Threads.

      
How long does it take? Usually a couple of minutes. When lots of people are saving, or Skwirl's processing computer is offline, it can take a few hours. Nothing gets lost, it waits in line, and you'll see a banner if it's slow.

      
Limits. 25 saves waiting at once, and 30 a day on the shared AI allowance. Add your own free Groq key above and it's 200 a day.

      
Why did a save fail? Usually the post is private or deleted, or YouTube blocked the download. Tap Retry once. If it fails again, remove it.

      
Who can see my saves? Only you, in the app. But whoever runs Skwirl can read the database. Don't save anything you'd hate for them to see.

      
Leaving? Export your notes or delete your account under Your account. Deleting is permanent.
