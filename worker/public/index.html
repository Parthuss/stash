What’s an access token?

Stash is invite-only for now. Your token is a private password that starts with stk_. It’s how Stash knows which saves are yours.

  1. Got an invite link? Open it. You're signed in, nothing to paste.
  2. Got a message with a token? Paste it above.
  3. Got an invite code? Use the Join box.
  4. Neither? Ask the person who told you about Stash to send you an invite.

Keep your token private. Anyone who has it can see your saves. You can find it again later in Set up → Your account.

Good to know
Limits, timing, privacy

    

      
What can I save? Links from Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X and Threads.

      
How long does it take? A minute or two. Longer when Stash is busy, and you'll see a banner when it is.

      
Limits. 25 saves waiting at once, 200 a day. The AI that reads reels has a free daily allowance shared by everyone. Add your own Groq key above to get your own.

      
Why did a save fail? Usually the post is private or deleted, or YouTube blocked the download. Tap Retry once. If it fails again, remove it.

      
Who can see my saves? Only you, in the app. But whoever runs Stash can read the database. Don't save anything you'd hate for them to see.

      
Leaving? Export your notes or delete your account under Your account. Deleting is permanent.
