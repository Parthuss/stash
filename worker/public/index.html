Questions

What is Skwirl?

Skwirl turns the Instagram reels and videos you save into notes you can search. Share a reel to Skwirl and a couple of minutes later it's a note: recipes come back as ingredients and steps, books, films and places land in one list, and tips come with the next step. Your saves also come back while you work: with the Skwirl plugin, Claude Code checks them on every message and brings in the one that fits, and ChatGPT or the Claude app can search them when you ask. It's free to try, and Skwirl Plus is there if you save a lot.

Can I search my saved Instagram reels?

Instagram's Saved tab has no search box for most people. Skwirl reads each reel you share to it (the speech, the on-screen text and the caption) and turns it into a note, so you can search everything you saved by what was said in it.

Can Claude use my saves while I work?

Yes. In Claude Code, the Skwirl plugin checks your saves on every message and brings in the ones that fit the task, like a design idea, a Claude skill or a GitHub repo you saved. In the Claude app and ChatGPT, connect Skwirl and they can search your saves while you work: coding, design, writing, research or planning a client session. They may not check every time, so ask them to check Skwirl, or add the one line from Set up to your Claude preferences. How to set it up.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone (through a Shortcut, Skwirl walks you through it), Android (install it from Chrome and it shows up in the share menu), and any browser, where you can paste links. You can also send links to the Skwirl bot on Telegram.

How long does a save take?

Usually a couple of minutes. You'll see it appear in your library with a "Reading…" card while it works.

Who can see my saves?

Other users cannot see your saves unless you share a note. The person running Skwirl can read the database, and Groq processes media to make your notes. Details in the privacy policy.

Can I bring in reels I saved before?

Yes. Download your Saved posts from Instagram (Accounts Center, Export your information, JSON format) and pick the file in Skwirl. It's read on your device and only the post links are sent. Step-by-step guide.

There's no password. How do I get back in?

Tap Sign in and enter your email: we send you a 6-digit code, and you're in on any phone or computer. Nothing to remember or reset.

What's a Groq key and do I need one?

No. Groq runs the AI that reads reels. Skwirl covers it for you. If you save a lot, a free key of your own gives you a separate AI allowance. Processing still uses the shared queue.

Good to know
Limits, timing, privacy

    

      
What can I save? Reels and videos from Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and X. TikTok and Threads aren't supported yet.

      
How long does it take? Usually a couple of minutes. When lots of people are saving, or Skwirl's processing computer is offline, it can take a few hours. Nothing gets lost, it waits in line, and you'll see a banner if it's slow.

      
Limits. Free: 10 new saves a month, plus your 25 latest old Instagram saves. Skwirl Plus: up to 250 a month read first, and your whole Saved folder. Up to 25 saves can wait at once.

      
Why did a save fail? Usually the post is private or deleted, or YouTube blocked the download. Tap Retry once. If it fails again, remove it.

      
Who can see my saves? Only you, in the app. But whoever runs Skwirl can read the database. Don't save anything you'd hate for them to see.

      
Leaving? Export your notes or delete your account from your profile (the round button at the top). Deleting is permanent.
