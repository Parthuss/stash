Terms

Last updated 27 September 2026. Short, because it's a pilot.

Stash is an invite-only test. It's free, it may break, and it may shut down. You can always export your notes first.

Using Stash

  • Save things you're allowed to look at: public posts. Stash is for your personal reference, not for re-publishing other people's content.
  • Don't try to get into anyone else's account, overload the service, or use it to fetch anything other than the supported social sites.
  • Keep your access token private. Anyone who has it can use your account.

What Stash promises, and doesn't

  • Notes are written by AI. They can be wrong, miss things, or misread a recipe. Check before you rely on them, especially quantities and anything to do with health or money.
  • There's no guarantee of uptime, speed, or that a particular post can be read. Some posts are private, deleted, or blocked from download.
  • Stash is provided as-is during the pilot, with no warranty.

Ending

  • You can delete your account any time from the app.
  • The owner may remove accounts that break these terms, or end the pilot. If the pilot ends, you'll get notice and a chance to export.
