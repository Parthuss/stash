# Owner console research — 12 November 2026

- [Stripe Dashboard](https://docs.stripe.com/dashboard/basics): keep performance, operational alerts, and customer management inside one predictable navigation; selecting a customer reveals detail instead of a second dashboard.
- [Plausible dashboard](https://plausible.io/docs/guided-tour) and [comparisons](https://plausible.io/docs/compare-stats): one visible period control, headline totals with previous-period context, and ranked Sources / Pages / Countries / Devices; borrow the quiet white canvas and purple emphasis seen in dashboard reference images.
- [Vercel Web Analytics](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/using-web-analytics): expose timeframe and data dimensions explicitly; traffic should answer where visitors came from and where they landed, without query-string clutter.
- [PostHog funnels](https://posthog.com/docs/product-analytics/funnels): define steps, order, and conversion window; show cohort boundaries and drop-off, never imply that unrelated anonymous visitors and new accounts form a proven conversion path.
- [Linear Insights](https://linear.app/docs/insights): choose the chart from the question and pair it with a table; use interaction to connect a summary with the underlying records.
- [Mixpanel Boards](https://docs.mixpanel.com/docs/boards): collect the important reports into one view and use short explanatory text to make the metrics tell a coherent story.
- [NN/g complex applications](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/complex-application-design/) and [data tables](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/data-tables/): reduce decoration, preserve capability, and support finding, comparing, inspecting, and acting on records through a table and detail drawer.
- Decision for Skwirl: light Rethink Sans / Recursive surface; ink text, lavender emphasis, thin single-scale lines and horizontal count bars, text labels plus hover details and table views; stack overview → funnel → users → health → traffic → activity at 390px.
- Visual inspection: opened [Plausible’s own dashboard image](https://plausible.io/assets/images/privacy-focused-web-analytics.webp): compact KPI strip with dividers, shared date control, one thin lavender trend line, fine horizontal rules, right-aligned counts and proportional row bars beneath. Take this hierarchy, omit its area fade per Skwirl’s no-gradient brief. Mixpanel’s hosted reference-image fetch returned 403.
