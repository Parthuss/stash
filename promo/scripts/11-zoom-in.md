# Piece 11: "Girl, zoom in" (carousel, 5 slides, script before build)

**Trend:** "Girl, zoom in" (NewEngen Nov 2026 list; SocialBee 21 Oct). Slide 1 is a pinch pose with
"girl, zoom in...", the next slides reward pinching in with hidden detail. Format trends run 3-4 weeks, so
post by about 18 Nov or skip it.
**Why it fits Skwirl:** the joke IS the product pain, but told through play instead of a lecture. A Saved
folder is literally a dense grid of tiny tiles nobody looks at closely. We make people look closely, and
every tile is a small, specific, true-to-life joke. People comment what they found and send it to the
friend whose Saved folder it describes. That's sends + comments + dwell time, the three signals that count.
**Different from earlier pieces:** no voice, no "graveyard" lecture, no app tour. It's a game.
**Bucket:** relatable (the empty one).
