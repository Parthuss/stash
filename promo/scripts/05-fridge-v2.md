# Piece 5 v2: "11:40pm. Fridge open." (script before build)

**Target:** 32s Reel, 1080×1920, 30fps, music on a 120 BPM grid (1 beat = 15 frames). Faceless, fully animated.
**One idea:** the reel you saved is lost in Instagram; Skwirl gives it back as a note you can use.
**Open loop (asked at 0s, answered at ~19s):** will he find the pasta recipe?

**Risks and how v2 handles them**
- 32s is longer than brag's 18–22s sweet spot. It's earned only if every beat moves: beats 3 and 4 are the explanation v1 lacked, so they stay; nothing else repeats.
- "Built like Instagram" must stay a recognisable layout, not their branding: no logo, no wordmark, no real creators.
- The flashback must read instantly: a hard "3 months ago" stamp and a sepia/desaturated tint, so nobody thinks it's the present.
