# Piece 12: "No. No. No. Yes." (Reel, ~13s, loop, script before build)

**Trend:** "No, no, no... Yes!" (NewEngen Nov 2026 list). Three or four dull clips each stamped "no", then
the good one stamped "yes" and held a beat longer. Under ~12s plus a short end card. The trending sound has
unclear business-safe status, so we bake our own audio: the joke is carried by the text and the stamps.
**One idea:** every way you try to find a reel you saved is bad, except typing what you remember.
**Why it fits Skwirl:** the format IS a comparison, and only the "yes" needs the product. Short, so it
completes and loops (completion and rewatches drive Reels).
**Different from earlier pieces:** not a night POV, not a list, not a launch tour. Fast, punchy, comedic.
**Bucket:** educational/demo with a meme frame.
